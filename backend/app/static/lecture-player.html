Privacy & Learning Assistance Guarantee

100% Local & Privacy-Preserving Architecture:

  • No Video Recording: Camera frames are converted to low-resolution grayscale in volatile browser memory, processed for head pose, and immediately discarded.
  • No Server Uploads: Live camera video or frames are NEVER sent over the network or saved in databases.
  • No Audio Recording: Microphone samples are used solely for real-time decibel/RMS volume calculation and instantly deleted. No speech transcription or voice identity is performed.
  • No Facial Recognition: CogniTrace only checks whether a face is oriented toward the screen—we do not identify individual faces.
  • Derived Analytics Only: Only numerical attention states (e.g. focused duration, distraction score) are used to provide gentle feedback.
  • Complete User Control: You can pause or disable monitoring at any time.
